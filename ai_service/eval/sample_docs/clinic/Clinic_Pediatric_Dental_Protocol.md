# Pediatric Dental Protocol (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL - testing only)

## Consent
A parent or legal guardian must sign consent before any procedure on a patient under 18 years.

## Preventive care
- Fluoride varnish every 6 months for children at moderate or high caries risk.
- Pit-and-fissure sealants on permanent molars soon after they erupt: first molars at about 6-7 years, second molars at about 11-13 years.

## Behaviour management
Use tell-show-do. Keep first visits short. Do not use physical restraint.
