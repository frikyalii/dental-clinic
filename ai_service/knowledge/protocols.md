# Clinic protocols (sample - replace with your own)

## Root canal treatment - aftercare
- Numbness lasts 2-4 hours; avoid chewing until it wears off to prevent biting the lip or cheek.
- Mild soreness for 2-3 days is normal; take the prescribed pain relief as directed.
- Avoid hard or sticky food on the treated side until the permanent filling or crown is placed.
- Do not skip the next session; an open temporary filling can become infected.

## Tooth extraction - aftercare
- Bite on the gauze for 30-45 minutes. Do not rinse, spit forcefully or use a straw for 24 hours.
- Cold, soft food on the first day. No smoking or tobacco for at least 48 hours.
- From the next day, rinse gently with warm salt water after meals.
- Some oozing is normal on day one. Severe pain after day 3 may indicate dry socket.

## Filling - aftercare
- Sensitivity to hot and cold for a few days is common. Avoid chewing until numbness is gone.

## When the patient should call the clinic
- Swelling of the face or neck, fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing (go to emergency care).
- Bleeding that does not stop after 30 minutes of firm pressure.
- Pain that is getting worse instead of better after 3 days.

## Medication cautions
- Always ask about allergies before prescribing antibiotics or painkillers.
- Patients on blood thinners need extra care with NSAIDs and metronidazole.
